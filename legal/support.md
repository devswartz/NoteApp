# Support

{{APP_NAME}} is a notes and tasks app for the web, Windows, Mac, iPhone, iPad and Android. This page explains how to get help.

## Contact us

Email [{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}](mailto:{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}). We usually reply within two working days. It helps to include which device you use, the app version (Settings → About) and what you were doing when the problem happened.

You can also send feedback from inside the app: press **?** (or search the command palette for "feedback") and choose **Send feedback**.

## Common questions

**I forgot my password.** On the sign-in screen, choose **Forgot password?** and we'll email you a link to reset it.

**How do I share a page or a notebook?** Open the page and choose **Share** at the top. You can share a single page with anyone by email, as view only or with editing. People in your workspace can also share whole notebooks.

**How do I get my data out?** Go to Settings → Profile → **Export my data** to download your pages, tasks and files.

**How do I delete my account?** Go to Settings → Profile → **Delete account**. Your account and personal data are removed straight away. Backups age out within about three months, as explained in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

**The desktop app didn't update.** It checks for updates every time it opens. You can also download the latest version from [github.com/devswartz/NoteApp/releases/latest](https://github.com/devswartz/NoteApp/releases/latest).
